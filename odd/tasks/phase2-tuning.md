## Assumptions

Neither the user nor the brief states these.

- **A1.** The phase 1 holdout (one of five polygon folds, seed 42) stays untouched as the test set, so the phase 1 and phase 2 results are comparable.
- **A2.** "More folds, balanced by class" is implemented as `StratifiedGroupKFold`, 5 folds × 2 seeds = 10 validations. 10 single-seed folds were rejected (see L2).
- **A3.** Random Forest and Gradient Boosting are tuned on a class-stratified 25 % subsample that keeps the same folds, then refit on the full training set. This keeps the run time and memory within limits: the container has about 8 GB.
- **A4.** The spectral indices are part of the search space for Logistic Regression and Naive Bayes only. Adding them to the tree grids would double their cost.
- **A5.** "The same notebook" is `02_phase2_tuning.ipynb`: KNN and the MLP join `SEARCHES`, so they share the holdout, the 10 validations, the macro-F1 objective, the refit and the metrics with the other models. The whole notebook is re-executed so every model comes from one run.
- **A6.** KNN and the MLP are tuned on the same 25 % subsample as RF and GB, then refit on the full training set. KNN was first searched on the full training set with 80 candidates; the run was stopped by the user after 3 h 18 min (L15).
- **A7.** The MLP always receives log-bands plus indices (the best preprocessing of LR and NB). `class_weight` is passed through `ClassWeightedClassifier` as sample weights. A (256, 128) network was dropped: one fit on the subsample took more than 10 minutes. (64, 32) was dropped to shorten the run (L15).
